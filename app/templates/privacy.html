Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Berlari is a free, open-source app that builds a running training plan from your Strava activities. This page explains what it stores and how to remove it.

What we read from Strava

When you connect, you grant read-only access (read, activity:read_all). We read your name and your running activities: date, distance, moving time, elevation gain, heart rate and per-kilometre splits. We never write to your Strava account.

What we store

  • Your Strava athlete ID, name and access tokens (to keep you signed in and receive new runs).
  • A summary of each run (the numbers listed above, plus the Strava activity ID).
  • Your training plan, race details and the coach notes, reviews and fitness summaries generated for you.
  • Age, height and weight if you enter them, used only to set heart-rate ranges and how gently the plan builds up. They are not sent to Groq.

Data is stored on a single server operated by the Berlari maintainer. Traffic passes through Cloudflare.

Who sees it

Only you. Your data is never shown to other users, sold, or used for advertising.

To write coach notes, we send training numbers only (distances, paces, heart rate, plan details, no name or Strava ID) to Groq, which runs the language model. Your data is not used to train AI models by Berlari.

Deleting your data

  • Use Delete my data on the New plan page. Everything about you is removed immediately.
  • Or revoke Berlari in your Strava settings (My Apps). Strava tells us and we delete your data automatically.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/juanjosua/berlari.
