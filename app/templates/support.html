Common questions

"Strava login was cancelled or failed." Try again and tap Authorize on the Strava screen. Berlari can only connect a limited number of athletes for now; if it's full, please check back later.

My plan doesn't match my level. The plan starts from your last 12 weeks of runs on Strava. If you recently started logging runs, create a new plan after a few weeks of data.

A run isn't showing on my plan. New runs arrive automatically a minute or two after they reach Strava. Only runs (not walks or rides) on a day of your plan are matched.

When do coach notes appear? Each day's note is written the evening before (20:00 Jakarta time). Your condition summary updates each evening after days you trained.

How do I delete my data? Use "Delete my data" on the New plan page, or disconnect Berlari in your Strava settings. See the privacy policy.
